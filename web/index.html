Drum kit

Load VGM songs in the Library to find a game's sampled drums. MIDI channel 10 plays them, C2 the first.

PSG shapes

The volume shapes the game gave its square waves and noise. Program change on channels 7 to 11 picks one.

Songs

Load VGM files in the Library. Keep this tab open while a song plays. Play streams the song and sounds exactly as the console did. Play MIDI turns it into MIDI notes and patch CCs and plays it in GenMDM mode, close but not exact (send the kit from Drums for its drums). Save .mid saves that MIDI, in GenMDM's layout, for a DAW.

Help

Getting started

  1. Install the OPN-1 firmware on the FM-1: see installing the firmware.
  2. Plug the FM-1 in with USB and open this page in Chrome or Edge. Allow MIDI when the browser asks.
  3. In the Library, drop .opm patch files or VGM songs, tick the patches you want, pick a bank and slot, and press Send to FM-1.
  4. Turn PRESETS on the FM-1 to step through them, and play. The Editor changes the selected patch.
  5. To play several patches at once, see Setup: Layer stacks them on the keys, Split gives each MIDI channel its own. Until then the FM-1 plays one patch, on MIDI channels 1 to 6 and 12 to 15.

Installing OPN-1 is done at your own risk. It comes with no warranty, and its authors accept no responsibility for damaged or unusable devices.

MIDI port

MIDI layout

Normal plays as the Setup tab says. GenMDM: MIDI channels 1 to 6 are FM channels 1 to 6, one note each, as GenMDM (Play MIDI in Songs uses it while it plays). Panel: every MIDI channel plays whatever is selected on the FM-1.

On the FM-1

ControlWhat it does
SELECTPicks what the keys and knobs play: FM, PSG 1 to 3, NOISE or DRUMS (with a layer or split, PART 1 to 6 instead of FM)
PRESETSSteps through the library's patches, skipping empty slots
ALGORITHMHow the four operators connect
HOME, LFO, FX, ARPWhat the four knobs edit
EDIT, ENVOne operator large, or all four envelopes. Again for the next operator
GLOVelocity, bend, MIDI setup or layout, tuning. Again: SETUP, the parts of a layer or split
SAVEKeeps the edited patch in its slot, and the setup
SEQ, REC, PLAYThe channel monitor, grab what is playing, stop a song
