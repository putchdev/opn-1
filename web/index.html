Changes play on the FM-1 as you make them. Save writes the patch to its slot and keeps it. Computer keys play too: Z to M and Q to U.

Parts

Six FM parts share the six FM voices. Parts on the same MIDI channel play together, so one can be a sound's attack, another its body, and a Release part its tail.

Drum kit

Load VGM songs in the Library to find a game's sampled drums. MIDI channel 10 plays them, C2 the first.

PSG shapes

The volume shapes the game gave its square waves and noise. Program change on channels 7 to 11 picks one.

Songs

Load VGM files in the Library. A song streams to the FM-1 while it plays, so keep this tab open.

Help

Getting started

  1. Install the OPN-1 firmware on the FM-1: see installing the firmware.
  2. Plug the FM-1 in with USB and open this page in Chrome or Edge. Allow MIDI when the browser asks.
  3. In the Library, drop .opm patch files or VGM songs, tick the patches you want, pick a bank and slot, and press Send to FM-1.
  4. Turn PRESETS on the FM-1 to step through them. Parts sets which MIDI channel plays what.

Installing OPN-1 is done at your own risk. It comes with no warranty, and its authors accept no responsibility for damaged or unusable devices.
